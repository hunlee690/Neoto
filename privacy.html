Privacy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This page explains how Neoto uses browser storage and online services to run the game. It will be updated before any new advertising or analytics service is enabled.

Game and browser data

Neoto may store settings, a local player name, locally earned progress and equipped items in your browser. Clearing browser data, using private browsing or changing devices can remove local-only progress.

Online character data

The game loads its character directory and world data from online services. Eligible NPC achievements and rewards may be stored remotely so the character can keep that progress across supported devices. Anonymous authentication identifiers may be used to secure permitted updates.

Advertising

Advertising is currently disabled in the website wrapper. If Google advertising is enabled later, Google and its partners may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses or similar identifiers to provide, measure and protect ads. Where required, Neoto will request consent through a Google-certified consent management platform before personalized advertising is served.

You can learn how Google uses information from partner sites at Google’s partner-sites information page and manage Google ad personalization at Google Ads Settings.

Hosting and security

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Standard technical request information may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver the site and protect its services.

Your choices

You may clear this site’s browser data through your browser settings. If a consent message is available, you may revisit your privacy choices through the control provided on the site.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Neoto through the links on the contact page.
